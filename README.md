# The Culture Map Explorer 🧭

An interactive Vue 3 application visualizing the cultural frameworks, scales, and countries described in **Erin Meyer's** acclaimed book ***The Culture Map: Breaking Through the Invisible Boundaries of Global Business*** (INSEAD).

---

## 🌟 Features

1. **The 8 Behavioral Scales Explorer**:
   - **Communicating**: Low-Context vs. High-Context
   - **Evaluating**: Direct Negative Feedback vs. Indirect Negative Feedback
   - **Persuading**: Principles-First (Deductive) vs. Applications-First (Inductive)
   - **Leading**: Egalitarian vs. Hierarchical
   - **Deciding**: Consensual vs. Top-Down
   - **Trusting**: Task-Based (Cognitive) vs. Relationship-Based (Affective)
   - **Disagreeing**: Confrontational vs. Avoids Confrontation
   - **Scheduling**: Linear-Time vs. Flexible-Time
   - Interactive spectrum bars with country pins, tooltips, and Erin Meyer's rules.

2. **30 Country Profiles**:
   - Deep-dive into each country (US, Japan, France, Germany, China, India, Netherlands, Israel, Brazil, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, etc.).
   - Interactive 8-dimension **Radar Chart**.
   - Distinctive cultural traits, famous quotes from the book, and workplace collaboration advice.

3. **Country Comparison & Friction Analyzer**:
   - Side-by-side gap analysis and superimposed dual radar chart.
   - Automatic detection of **Cultural Friction Hotspots** (where divergence is high).
   - Meyer's classic matchups (e.g. *US vs France*, *Netherlands vs US*, *Germany vs Japan*, *UK vs France*).

4. **The 4 Classic 2x2 Quadrants**:
   - *Communicating vs. Evaluating* (The Feedback Sandwich Trap)
   - *Leading vs. Deciding* (The Japanese Consensual Paradox)
   - *Disagreeing vs. Trusting* (Debate vs. Loss of Face)
   - *Persuading vs. Communicating* (Deductive vs Inductive vs Holistic)

5. **Multicultural Team Simulator**:
   - Build a custom international team.
   - Calculates team variance and dispersion across all 8 scales.
   - Generates an actionable **Team Working Agreement**.

6. **Key Book Concepts & Linguistic Translation**:
   - *Relative Positioning* (The Golden Rule)
   - *Upgraders vs. Downgraders* with real phrasing examples
   - *The Anglo-Saxon to European translation guide*
   - *Peach vs. Coconut cultures* (Kurt Lewin)
   - *Nemawashi & Ringisho*
   - *Holistic vs. Specific Cognition*

---
